# Presentation demo: presenter script (≈ 40 seconds)

**Route:** `#/demo` (e.g. `http://localhost:4176/#/demo`)

**What it is:**
- An isolated, fictional demo learner: Saraswathi, vegetable stall.
- One abbreviated diagnostic question, scored by the app's real assessment engine.
- The real coaching rules then produce the recommendation and the coach guidance.

**What it is not:** a full assessment. The screen says "Demo check · 1 question". The example question is **illustrative**: it is inspired by the needs in The/Nudge's concept note, and was not requested by them.

## Before you present (2 minutes)

1. **Use desktop Chrome or Edge, online.** In Chrome, the browser's speech recognition sends audio to the browser vendor's service. The app itself does not store or send it.
2. Open `#/demo` and click **Reset demo** (top right).
3. **Test the voice.**
   - Choose English, tap **🔊 Listen** and confirm you hear the question.
   - Tap the microphone and say "yes". Allow microphone access when the browser asks.
   - Then click **Reset demo** again.
4. Turn the volume up. Use a quiet room, or a headset microphone if the hall is noisy.
5. Keep the slide with the tap fallback in mind: if the microphone fails, **tap "Yes"**. The app labels it "Tapped", so do not say it was voice.

## Script

| Time | Do | Say (suggested) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 s | Language screen is showing. Point at the six cards, then click **English** | "Saraswathi runs a vegetable stall. She starts in her own language. Three languages work today; Bengali, Assamese and Tamil are next." |
| 7–24 s | The question is read aloud automatically (after your click). Tap the **microphone** and say **"Yes."** The screen shows *Heard: "yes" → Yes*. Tap **Use this answer**, then **Confirm answer** | "She hears the question and answers by voice. No reading needed. She says yes: ₹500 in sales means ₹500 profit." |
| 24–28 s | Recommendation card: Sales and profit · Foundational · ₹500 − ₹300 = ₹200 *before other expenses*. Click **View Coach Guidance** | "Her own answer shows she may be confusing sales with profit, so she gets that lesson, not a generic course." |
| 28–40 s | Coach View: point at 1 → 2 → 3 | "Her field coordinator sees why this lesson, the exact answer behind it, what she struggled with, and what to reinforce at the next visit: a ₹500 / ₹300 picture and one question to ask." |

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No audio | The question is on screen in large text. Say "the app reads it aloud on her phone", and keep going |
| Microphone blocked, or "Speech not available" | Tap **Yes**. It is labelled *Tapped* everywhere, including in the Coach View |
| The app heard something else | Tap **Try again**, or type the word into the "Heard" box. Nothing counts until you confirm |
| You said "repeat" by accident | It is not counted as an answer; the question is read again |
| Wrong stage | Use the numbered stage buttons in the top bar (3 and 4 unlock after an answer). **Reset demo** starts over |

**Optional (if asked "what if she says No?").** Go back to stage 2, answer **No**, then Confirm. The result changes: no misconception, the next topic is Everyday costs, and the level is "not yet known". This shows the result really depends on her answer.
